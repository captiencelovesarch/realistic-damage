Realistic Damage 2.0: Grounded sound tuner

Every Grounded sound here is synthesised live in this page. Drag a slider and it plays again, so you can tune each one by ear. New take rolls different random cracks and splats with the same settings.

When something sounds right (or you give up on one), press Copy all settings and paste it to Claude, with a few words on what still bothers you. Your sliders are remembered if you close the page. Reset goes back to the last settings Claude saved from you, not to Claude's first guess.

# Ko-fi setup — Realistic Damage

The Ko-fi is a tip jar for now. Paid early access only makes money once people
are already waiting for the next build, so it comes later (see the bottom).

## Sign-up (you do this part)

1. ko-fi.com → Sign up. Page name: `captience`, or `realisticdamage` if you want it mod-only.
2. Payments: connect **PayPal** or **Stripe**. Ko-fi asks during setup; you need
   one of them to receive anything.
3. Ko-fi signs new pages up to a 5% platform fee on every payment, tips included,
   unless you opt out. Look for it in the payment settings. The PayPal/Stripe
   processing fee applies either way.

## After it exists

Copy your page link (`ko-fi.com/<name>`) and paste it into Modrinth →
Settings → Links → Donation, platform "Ko-fi".

## Later: paid early access

Once the Modrinth page has a few thousand downloads and people ask "when's the
next one?", add a Ko-fi membership tier (about $3/month) that gets each alpha a
week or two before Modrinth. The mod is CC0, so a supporter can legally share an
early build. That's fine: people pay to support the mod and to get builds first,
not because the jar is locked.
